# The Smashed Prize Pumpkin: trails

Opening at The Greenhouse (24 turns): Big Bertha, the prize pumpkin, was smashed overnight. A greenhouse window is broken. The greenhouse key has been missing for days. Mr. Dobbs saw Bertha whole on his 8pm rounds. Ms. Hale found Bertha smashed at 7:30. Toby and Mia are the club's two pupils. Mr. Thorne is the rival grower next door, and he is being blamed.

## `nobody_broke_in`: a stepping stone

Nobody broke in through the window. Whoever did it came through the locked door with a key.

Rule: all of (clue_glass_outside)

### Trail 1 of 1

- `clue_glass_outside` (evidence; the broken side window, The Greenhouse): The broken glass from the side window lies outside on the brick path, not inside. The window was knocked out from inside the greenhouse, so nobody climbed in through it.
  - reached: search the broken side window
